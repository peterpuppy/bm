# Configuring Activities Based on Your Game’s Design

This chapter provides on overview on how to maximize the potential of PlayStation™Network Activities by creating them based on how you've designed your game.

When properly configured, activities have the potential to increase hours played and player retention for your game. This includes games from a variety of genres, such as linear single player story driven games and competitive multiplayer titles.

Follow the guidance for designing activities depending on how players interact with your game. You can create activities to complement most game designs:

* [Games with campaigns and story modes](configuring-campaign-or-story-modes.html "This chapter provides details on how to configure activities for games that feature campaign and story modes.")
* [Games with repeatable or endless modes](configuring-repeatable-or-endless-modes.html "This chapter provides guidance on configuring activities for games that feature repeatable or endless modes.")
* [Games with online multiplayer modes](configuring-multiplayer-activities.html "This chapter provides guidance on creating activities for games with online multiplayer modes.")
* [Games with post-release content](configuring-activities-for-post-release-content.html "This chapter provides guidance on creating activities for games that feature post-release content such as downloadable content and user generated content.")

SIE recommends that you follow the best practice guidelines that extend beyond the [TRC minimum configurations for campaigns (R5301)](trc-requirements-r5301.html "This topic provides information on the TRC requirements you must follow if your game has a campaign or story mode.") and [competitive multiplayer (R5302)](trc-requirements-r5302.html "This topic provides information on the TRC requirements you must follow if your game has competitive multiplayer modes.") activities to get the most out of the activities you create.

You can implement different configurations from what are described in this guide; however, activity UX/UI is optimized for activities that are created using the instructions in this guide.

If your game’s design is not represented or you have questions about how to make the most of the UDS system, file a ticket on DevNet.
