# Managing an Account in the Account Library

This section describes how to manage accounts in the Development Accounts Account Library.

If you already have an account on PlayStation™Network, you may add the account to the account library. Unlocking is not required to add the account, however unlocking is required to access protected properties of the account.

Note: At this time, Advanced Player Profile Editor does not require any additional unlocking, however this may change in the future. For most other tasks in Development Accounts, accounts cannot be edited until unlocked, as described elsewhere in this document.

To add an account, click the **Add Account** button. In the resulting dialog (see figure below), select a method to add the account. There are two methods you can use:

* Online ID. You can add an account if you have the online ID as it would be displayed on the client.
* Email Address (sign-in ID). You can add an account if you have the email.

# Adding Accounts from Other Places

This topic describes how to add accounts from other places into the Development Accounts Library.

There are other places where accounts can be added to the library. One of those places is DevNet's Title Developer and Title Administrator role management screen. Anytime an account is added to a title as either a Title Dev or Title Admin it also appears in your account library automatically.

# Removing Accounts from the Library

This topic describes how to remove accounts from the Development Accounts Library.

In the event you find that you no longer need an account in your library you can remove specific accounts from your library in a simple manner. Note that removing an account from your library does not close or delete the account from existence, it merely removes it from the library in Development Accounts.

To Remove an account:

1. In the table, click the Options menu (⋮) in line of the account.
2. Click **Remove from Library** in the list (see figure below).
3. A toast notification appears at the top of the screen indicating the account has been removed.

# Setting Communications Restriction

This topic describes set communication restrictions in the Development Accounts Library.

The Development Accounts Tool has the capability to test communications restriction. Communications restriction should limit a specific account for PlayStation™Network from being able to interact with other accounts in various social features.

To test communications restriction for an account:

1. Click the toggle button under the **Communications Suspension** column in the Account Library.
2. If the account is locked, unlock the account with the account's sign-in ID (email address) and password.
3. Click **Submit**.

This setting cannot be used for verification for communication restriction on PlayStation®4, PlayStation®Vita and PlayStation®3 because sign-in to PlayStation™Network with the setting is blocked on these platforms.

Note:

Accounts for PlayStation™Network with 2-step verification cannot be unlocked in the account library. Contact your developer support representative with any other account unlocking issues.

# Managing Title Privileges In Bulk

This topic describes the bulk handling of Title Privileges in the Development Accounts Library.

Development Accounts allows users to manage title privileges for accounts. To manage title privileges, click on the **Bulk Add Title Privileges** button in the upper left-hand corner of the Account Library table (see figure below) or click the Options menu (⋮) in the Library for an account you wish to manage.

Bulk Add Title Privileges Button

The **Add Title Privileges** dialog then appears with a list of your currently added accounts on one side and the titles you currently have access to on the other.

Add Title Privileges

Click the check boxes for the accounts and titles you wish to associate and then click **Save**.

A confirmation appears listing what accounts and titles have been successful associate or failed.

# Managing Title Privileges for a Single Account

This topic describes how to manage Title Privileges in the Development Accounts Library.

To manage title privileges for a single account, click the Options menu (⋮) in line with the account you wish to manage. The Manage Title Privileges dialog appears with a check box list of all title IDs the user has access to. Click the check boxes for the titles you wish to add or remove and then click **Save**.
