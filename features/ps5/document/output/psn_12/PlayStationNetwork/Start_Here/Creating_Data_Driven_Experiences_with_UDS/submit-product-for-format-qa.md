# Submit Product for Platform Certification and Operations Review

After you have tested, debugged, and finished development of your game, you must submit your release-ready product as a package file for Platform Certification and Operations review.

This release readiness does not pertain just to your UDS implementation. Rather, it
involves meeting several other requirements as well before your product is considered ready to
package for release and upload for Platform Certification and Operations review. As such, this
section is brief and serves as an indication that submission to Platform Certification and
Operations must occur. When you're satisfied that your product is ready, you can prepare it
for submission to Certification Center (formerly known as GFQAweb).

# Book a Slot

Before you create and submit your package for Platform Certification and Operations review you must book a testing slot for each submission.

Certain dates may be unavailable or have limited availability, so plan accordingly ahead of
time.

# Create and Submit Package

This topic provides an overview of the procedure for creating and submitting a package.

Using the same documents as noted under [Create the Package](create-the-package.html "This topic provides an overview of the procedure for internal QA testing of your data-driven experiences. This workflow assumes you are creating a release-ready version of your game's package file."), create your release-ready package file for your product. Next, use the Package/Disc
Management Tool (GEMS) to upload your package file for Platform Certification and Operations review. After uploading
your content, you must complete a Master Disc Testing (MDT) Form that includes master
data specifications.

The submission process typically entails submitting your product and then addressing
any checks that failed the Technical Requirements Checklist (TRC). Before you submit
your product, consider reviewing the TRC sections noted below, which pertain to UDS
implementation.

# Sign Liability Form

This topic links to information about signing liability forms after your products pass Platform Certification and Operations checks.

Once your products pass all of the Platform Certification and Operations checks, you must sign a liability form
within Certification Center (formerly known as GFQAweb).
