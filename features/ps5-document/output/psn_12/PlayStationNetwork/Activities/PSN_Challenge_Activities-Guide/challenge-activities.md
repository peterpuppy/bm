# PlayStation™Network Challenge Activities Overview

This chapter provides an overview of PlayStation™Network Challenge Activities.

Challenge activities display rankings in leaderboard format based on the outcome of asynchronous gameplay results. Leaderboards are shown at the platform level and the system generates appropriate cohorts (global and friends-only) according to user-specific data.

Examples of activities that can be created as challenges are:

* *Uncharted* speed runs.
* *Astro Play Room* Mountain run.
* *Astro* Ain't Seen Nothing Yeti.
