# The Account Library

This topic describes the Development Accounts Account Library.

The Account Library is a list of accounts on the PlayStation™Network development environment that have been associated to your PlayStation™Partners account.

Development Accounts allows you to associate this group of accounts to your PlayStation™Partners account. Accounts in your library are made available in other account-based features such as Advanced Player Profile using the account selector.

By default, accounts are locked when added to the library, and certain operations may not be possible until account is unlocked (see [Unlocking Accounts in the Library](unlocking-accounts-in-the-library.html "This topic describes account unlocking in the Development Accounts Library.")).

When you first open Development Accounts, your library may be empty, if you have not already added to it from another account-based feature. You can add accounts to your library using the **Add to Library** button on the right of the screen. For more information see the [Managing an Account in the Account Library](managing-an-account-in-the-account-library.html "This section describes how to manage accounts in the Development Accounts Account Library.") section.

# Account Selector

This topic describes how use the Account Selector in Development Accounts.

In some account-based features, an account from the Account Library (see figure below) can be selected using the account selector menu.

The account selector displays a list of accounts in your library. Selecting an account from the list sets the account context to the selected account, allowing you to change the account being edited without having to return to the Account Library page.

When you add an account to the library from within an editor, the newly added account is added to the list of accounts available within the account selector, and the newly added account is selected as the account context.

# Library Search

This topic describes how to do a search in the Account Library.

The account library can be searched by sign-in ID, online ID, title ID and locale. To search, type in the search parameters you need into the search box in the upper right-hand corner and press the enter key. To clear your search, empty the search box and press the enter key again. This resets the search criteria in the table list view.
